# Privacy Policy — DuoVision Pro

**Effective date:** September 19, 2026

DuoVision Pro ("the app", "we", "us") is a local media player. This policy explains what happens with your videos, photos, and settings — which is: not much, and never off your device.

## The short version

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your information. The app plays media stored on your device and keeps your preferences on your device.

## What data the app collects

None. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network connections and contains no servers, so there is nothing it could upload your data to.

## Your videos and photos

- Videos and photos are selected with the iOS system picker and played directly from your device's storage inside the app.
- The app does not copy your library, scan it, or index it. It touches only the items you explicitly choose for each pane.
- Nothing you play in the app is uploaded, shared, or made available to anyone else.

## Your settings

- Per-video preferences — playback position, volume, and speed — are saved locally on your device using iOS's standard settings storage. They never leave the device and are removed if you delete the app.

## Permissions the app requests and why

- **Photos:** Used only so you can pick videos and photos to load into a pane. The app reads only the items you select and does not modify your library.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, Bluetooth, or Local Network access, and has no use for them.

## Children's privacy

The app collects no data from any user of any age, so no personal information is collected from children under 13.

## Third parties

No third-party services, ad networks, or analytics providers are embedded, so no information about you can reach third parties through the app.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the release notes before that version ships. The local-only, no-collection design described here applies to version 1.0.
